# Mula — Mulai aja.

First video edit based on the supplied 65-second brief. Rendered locally with animated vector shapes, typography, documentary photographs and an original synthesized instrumental score. No voice-over.

## Deliverables

- `mula-manifesto-1080p120.mp4`: complete picture and stereo soundtrack.
- `review-contact-sheet.jpg`: composition overview.
- `verification.txt`: measured final export properties.
- `brief.md`: supplied brief, preserved verbatim.
- `render.swift`, `make_audio.py`, `finish.swift`: editable production sources.

## Editorial choices

The founder montage remains removed. The hook is “Mulai aja.” Mula appears only at the close. The chart compares documented daily-order milestones rather than revenue; its dates are categorical and it does not invent intermediate measurements. The chart's baseline is zero, but the first observed data point is 3,000.

The available authentic documentary photos are dated 2018 and 2022 and labeled accordingly. They are not presented as photographs taken in 2010. This cut uses animated still photography and motion graphics rather than archival moving footage. The score is newly synthesized for this edit, not extracted from the reference video. No cloned voice or fabricated documentary footage is used.
